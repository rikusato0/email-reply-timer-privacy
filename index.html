Privacy Policy for WLAI Timer

Published as the privacy policy for the Chrome Web Store extension “Email Reply Timer” (also referred to as WLAI Timer).
Effective Date: June 1, 2026 · Developer: White Lotus AI

Overview

WLAI Timer is a Chrome extension designed for internal workplace productivity use. The extension helps users track the amount of time spent working on emails and, when the user chooses, record that time to ClickUp.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension may access, how that information is used, and the limits of our responsibility.

Information the Extension May Access

Depending on the user’s browser permissions, account configuration, and use of the extension, the extension may access or process limited information necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • The fact that a user has started or stopped working on an email-related task;
  • Time-tracking information, such as start time, stop time, duration, and related metadata;
  • Information entered or selected by the user when submitting time to ClickUp;
  • Limited webpage or browser information necessary to detect when the extension should start, stop, or display the time-tracking interface;
  • Authentication, workspace, task, or project information made available through ClickUp or related integrations, if authorized by the user or organization;
  • The text of the email reply or thread the user is working on, where the user enables the AI summary feature (see “OpenAI and AI Processing” below).

The extension is not intended to collect email contents for independent use by the developer. However, because the extension operates in connection with email-related workflows, it may technically interact with pages or interfaces where email-related information is displayed, and email text may be transmitted to the third-party services described below in order to provide the extension’s features.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the extension is used only for the following purposes:

  • To operate the timer;
  • To allow users to review, confirm, and submit tracked time;
  • To send user-approved time entries to ClickUp;
  • To generate an optional summary of the email being worked on, via OpenAI, for inclusion in the ClickUp record;
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the extension;
  • To comply with applicable legal, security, or platform requirements.

The extension does not sell user data.

OpenAI and AI Processing

When the AI summary feature is used, the text of the email reply or thread the user is working on is transmitted to OpenAI (api.openai.com) to generate a short summary. That summary is then attached to the corresponding ClickUp record.

This information is subject to OpenAI’s own terms and privacy policy. White Lotus AI is not responsible for OpenAI’s systems, security, privacy practices, availability, data retention, or processing of information after it is transmitted to OpenAI. Email text is sent to OpenAI only to provide the summary feature and is not used for advertising, sale of data, or unrelated profiling.

ClickUp, OpenAI, and Third-Party Services

If a user records time to ClickUp, the information submitted is sent to ClickUp and is subject to ClickUp’s own terms, privacy policy, permissions, and account settings.

White Lotus AI is not responsible for the systems, security, privacy practices, availability, data retention, or processing of information by ClickUp, OpenAI, or any other third-party service after it is submitted to those services.

Data Storage and Transmission

The extension may store limited operational data locally in the user’s browser or transmit information to authorized services as needed for the extension to function.

White Lotus AI uses commercially reasonable efforts to handle information securely, but no software, browser extension, network, or third-party service can be guaranteed to be error-free, uninterrupted, or completely secure.

Limited Use Disclosure

The extension’s use of information received from Chrome APIs is limited to providing and improving the extension’s stated functionality. The extension does not use Chrome API data for advertising, sale of data, creditworthiness determinations, or unrelated profiling.

User Control

Users control when they submit time entries to ClickUp. Users may disable or uninstall the extension through Chrome at any time. Organization administrators may also manage access, permissions, and configuration depending on how the extension is deployed.

Data Retention

The extension retains only the information reasonably necessary for its operation, troubleshooting, legal compliance, or legitimate business purposes. Data submitted to ClickUp is retained according to ClickUp’s policies and the settings of the applicable ClickUp account or workspace.

Organizational Use

If the extension is provided through an employer, client, or organization, that organization may have access to time entries, activity records, ClickUp submissions, or related work information according to its own internal policies and systems. Users should contact their organization with questions about workplace monitoring, employee records, or internal data practices.

No Guarantee of Accuracy

The extension is a productivity and time-tracking aid. It may not always capture time perfectly, detect every activity correctly, or submit information without error. Users are responsible for reviewing time entries before submitting them.

Changes to This Policy

White Lotus AI may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted where this Privacy Policy is made available. Continued use of the extension after an update means the updated policy applies.
